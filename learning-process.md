Feynman Technique is a learning method which says you can deeply understand a concept by learning to teach it simply

## Question 3
What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
### Answer 3
- Active mode: It is the concentrated mode of thinking where you use familiar methods to solve a problem
- Diffuse mode: It is the relaxed mode of thinking where your mind has to make new connections to solve the problem

## Question 4
According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.
### Answer 4
- Think about the problem in diffuse mode 
- When a get a good understanding about, take it into focus mode to refine your understanding
