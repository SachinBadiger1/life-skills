## Question 4: Paraphrase the Sleep is Your Superpower video in your own words in brief.
Sleep is essential for the brain and body because it improves memory, learning, focus, recovery, and overall health. Lack of sleep can negatively affect concentration, mood, immunity, and long-term health.

## Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?
Maintain a fixed sleep schedule, get enough sleep, avoid caffeine late in the day, reduce screen time before bed, and keep the room cool and dark. A relaxing bedtime routine can also improve sleep quality.

## Question 6: Paraphrase the video – Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise.
Exercise improves mood, focus, memory, and learning by positively affecting the brain. Regular exercise also strengthens areas of the brain involved in decision-making and memory and helps protect brain health.

## Question 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?
Start with small workouts and make exercise part of the daily routine. Walking, cycling, running, dancing, or any enjoyable activity can help build a regular exercise habit.
