Grit means staying focused on a goal and continuing to work even when things become difficult or you face failure.

## Q2. Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

A growth mindset means believing that abilities can improve through effort, practice, learning, and feedback.

## Q3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal locus of control means believing that your actions and choices influence your results. The key point is to focus on what you can control.
