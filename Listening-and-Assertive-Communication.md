## Q1. Steps for active listening:

- Give full attention to the speaker.
- Avoid interrupting while they are speaking.
- Ask questions when something is unclear.
- Pay attention to their words and feelings.
- Repeat important points to confirm understanding.
- Avoid judging before hearing the complete message.

## Q2. Fisher's model:

Listen carefully, understand the message and feelings, repeat the main points in simple words, and confirm the understanding.

## Q3. Obstacles in listening:

- Getting distracted during the conversation.
- Thinking about the reply instead of listening.
- Interrupting the speaker.
- Making assumptions too quickly.
- Losing focus during long conversations.

## Q4. How can listening be improved?

Avoid distractions, listen without interrupting, ask questions, and confirm important points.

## Q5. When does passive communication happen?

Passive communication happens when someone stays quiet or agrees even when they actually disagree.

## Q6. When does aggressive communication happen?

Aggressive communication happens when someone is angry or frustrated and speaks harshly or raises their voice.

## Q7. When does passive-aggressive communication happen?

Passive-aggressive communication happens when someone is upset but does not say it directly and instead uses sarcasm, taunts, or silent treatment.

## Q8. How can communication become assertive?

Speak clearly and calmly, express needs directly, listen to others, and respect their opinions.
